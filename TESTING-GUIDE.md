# Testing Guide

Use these files in order:

1. `FINAL-SETUP-GUIDE.md` — install and connect Supabase, email invitations, encryption key, and hosting.
2. `ADMIN-PILOT-TESTING.md` — test Owner/Admin, Licensee, Employee, location RLS, audit, and encrypted documents with sample data.
3. `PRODUCTION-READINESS-CHECKLIST.md` — final approval before entering real information.

Do not enter real child, family, medical, employee, KidKare, Timesheet, or subsidy information until the complete production-readiness checklist passes.
