# The Hub product priorities

The goal is to make TCS daily work easier to complete and verify. Keep the green/gator identity, current features, invite-only family access, separate households and internal-only opening/closing reports. Use synthetic records for development.

## Implemented in the daily-work website release

- Staff location conversations with threaded replies, pinned announcements and explicit acknowledgments.
- A searchable family inbox with unread and awaiting-reply views.
- Dashboard follow-up across tasks, pickup handoffs and internal reports.
- Dated work-plan deadlines, shift checkpoints, clear completion criteria and staff review records.
- Parent home care summary, actionable shortcuts and a dedicated Messages tab.
- Faster tool discovery, bounded message requests, retained failed-send drafts and idempotent retries.

## Next product work

1. **Child accountability:** Finish and test the pending Brightwheel identity/import work in its existing branch. One child profile across locations unless custody/privacy requires separation. Preserve historical records and review ambiguous matches before merging them.
2. **Care entry speed:** Measure the current attendance, meal, rest and photo steps with staff; streamline the busiest actions and confirm every save without filling in records automatically.
3. **Communication follow-up:** Add full per-thread history pagination, assigned reply ownership, configurable reminders and opt-in team-message alerts. Keep emergency/direct-contact instructions clear, and respect quiet hours and guardian/location permissions.
4. **Shift ownership:** Bring opening/midday/closing checks, three concrete priorities, assigned staff, due times and supervisor verification together. Keep the familiar report sections and their location-wide format.
5. **Family scheduling:** Strengthen the existing calendar around Friday 6 PM deadlines, closures, early submissions, late warnings and explicit location approval/capacity decisions.
6. **Forms and administration:** Improve missing-document and signature follow-through while retaining required official forms and the existing timesheet submission sets and evidence controls.
7. **Performance and accessibility:** Check on physical iPhones and slower connections, measure tap count and save time, and fix keyboard, screen-reader and contrast gaps found in the workflows above.
8. **Next native release:** Bundle the accepted website improvements into the next iPhone version, verify on real devices and prepare it after the current Apple review. Keep APNs, QR/camera, biometric unlock and account/privacy access working.

## How to judge improvements

For each workflow, compare before/after task time, taps, missed handoffs, unanswered messages, incomplete forms and save failures. Use observed TCS results rather than competitor feature counts. Pilot new actions with authorized test accounts before broad operational use.
