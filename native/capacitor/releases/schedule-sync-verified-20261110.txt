WHAT CHANGED
- The native export now explicitly adapts Child Schedules to WeeklyCarePlanner and Ratios to DailyRatioPlan, the same dated planner components used by the website. It resolves date/location links on the device and keys the planner by the selected date/location. It does not freeze the schedule date at package-build time or substitute the older recurring-only grid.
- The export copy alone removes request-time language-cookie access. The website's source layout, client language switch and staff language synchronization are retained. Unexpected layout/route shapes stop the export for review.
- The shared dated-plan reader reloads on foreground visibility, focus, page restoration, network return and realtime subscription recovery. Visible online readers also refresh every 30 seconds as a missed-event fallback.
- All five reads share the existing 12-second timeout/abort signal. Offline or failed reads return an error with ready=false, not an apparently current regular-hours fallback.
- Older requests cannot overwrite newer requests or another account/week, and unmounted/disabled readers do not expose old data.
- One-day overrides remain separate from usual weekly hours. No live schedule data, account permissions or security policies were changed.
